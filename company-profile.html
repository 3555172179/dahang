About ZGS Glass

Zibo Great Sailing Light Industrial Products Co., Ltd. (ZGS Glass) was founded in 2004 in Zibo, Shandong. We manufacture custom glassware for brand owners, retailers, and hospitality groups in 62 countries. Our product categories include glass jars & storage, drinkware, barware, vases & home decor, perfume & cosmetic packaging, tableware & kitchenware, and garden & outdoor glassware.

OEM and ODM projects run through a single production system using lead-free, high-white glass. Our factory is BSCI-audited, and products are tested to FDA and LFGB food-contact standards. We customize shape, capacity, and decoration, from a label refresh to a fully bespoke mold.

Production runs across 16 lines and outputs 1,200,000 pieces a day. One line is dedicated to recycled glass, which remelts crushed cullet into new glassware.

Every order ships with per-lot inspection reports, and a dedicated account manager covers each account end to end. Inquiries receive a reply within 24 hours.

Social Value

 

Glass manufacturing carries a responsibility to the environment and the communities around our plants. Here is how we hold ourselves to it.

Environmental Stewardship

Cullet from our own production returns to the furnace, and process water recirculates in a closed loop. A dedicated recycled-glass line takes cullet as its raw material, turning recovered glass into new ware instead of waste.

  • Closed-loop cullet return and water recirculation
  • Dedicated recycled-glass line running on recycled cullet
  • Recycled glass products across 7 categories

Community Giving

We support the communities around our Zibo plants through annual donations, in-kind contributions, and staff volunteer time. Surplus glassware from sample and photo shoots goes to local schools and shelters.

  • Annual donations to 11 local schools and shelters
  • In-kind donations of sample and photo-shoot glassware
  • Ongoing funding and glassware donations to 4 local elderly care centres
