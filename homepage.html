About ZGS Glass

Founded in 2004, ZGS Glass (Zibo Great Sailing Light Industrial Products Co., Ltd.) is a leading custom glassware manufacturer and glassware factory China buyers trust. Our portfolio covers glass jars, glass vases, glass candle holders, glass beverage dispensers, glass mason jars and glass decanters — supplying brand owners, wholesalers, hotels, restaurants, beauty brands, food & beverage companies and home decor retailers across 100+ countries. We combine lead-free glassware production with hand-blown craftsmanship and borosilicate glass expertise.
  • 40+ R&D Specialists & IPD System
    40+ in-house designers and engineers run an Integrated Product Development (IPD) process — launching 3-5 new designs monthly and 10-15 frontier R&D projects each season.
  • 7-10 Day Samples Worldwide
    Standard samples delivered in 7-10 business days; custom 3D prototypes in 48 hours. Located in Zibo with 2-hour access to Qingdao Port, we ship to 100+ countries with reliable lead times.
