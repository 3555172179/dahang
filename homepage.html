About ZGS Glass

Founded in 2004, ZGS Glass (Zibo Great Sailing Light Industrial Products Co., Ltd.) is a leading custom glassware manufacturer and glassware factory China buyers trust. Our portfolio covers glass jars, glass vases, glass candle holders, glass beverage dispensers, glass mason jars and glass decanters — supplying brand owners, wholesalers, hotels, restaurants, beauty brands, food & beverage companies and home decor retailers across 62 countries. We combine lead-free glassware production with hand-blown craftsmanship and borosilicate glass expertise.
  • 12 R&D Specialists & IPD System
    12 in-house designers and engineers run an Integrated Product Development (IPD) process — launching 3 to 5 new designs monthly and 10 to 15 frontier R&D projects each season.
  • 7 to 10 Day Samples Worldwide
    Standard samples delivered within 7 to 10 business days; custom 3D prototypes in 48 hours. Located in Zibo with 2 hours access to Qingdao Port, we ship to 62 countries with reliable lead times.

Real voice from our B2B buyers

FDA documentation is easy to find. LFGB is where most vendors fail. Our first German shipment from a previous vendor got held at customs for six weeks because they sent a generic test report — one PDF covering their entire catalog. ZGS sent per-product LFGB reports for our hotel line. Every SKU had its own certificate. Customs cleared in three days.

Andreas K.

Germany · Hotel procurement director

I needed a 250ml stemless wine glass for my wine bar — short body, thin walls, lead-free crystal. Three Chinese factories told me to use their stock design. ZGS made me a custom mold for 8,000 pieces. The mold fee was reasonable and the glass quality comes close to Riedel at a third of the price. I now stock six of their SKUs.

Mathieu L.

France · Wine bar owner

We placed our second order with ZGS in early 2025. The first order in 2024 was clean. This one shipped three weeks late and about a third of the jars had color mismatch on the lid — some more ivory, some more tan, all supposed to match the same Pantone code we sent. Their QC caught it before shipment but by then we'd already lost the retail launch slot. Glass quality is solid. Lead time and color batch consistency need tightening.

Daniel R.

United States · Food packaging distributor

Shipping to São Paulo is 35 days minimum by sea. Our high season is December — restaurants need stock by then. The logistics manager here said it was the first time in five years we had zero breakage on a glass shipment from China.

Carlos S.

Brazil · Hospitality group buyer
