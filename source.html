About Us

Zibo Great Sailing Light Industrial Products Co., Ltd. is specialized in producing both machine made and hand blown glassware since 2004. Main products include various glass beverage dispenser, glass storage jar, glass mason jar, glass vase and glass candle holder, ect. Total 890 workers, factory covers an area of 5000m2, 200,000pcs output each day.
