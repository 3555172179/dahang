About ZGS Glass

Founded in 2004, ZGS Glass (Zibo Great Sailing Light Industrial Products Co., Ltd.) is a leading custom glassware manufacturer and glassware factory China buyers trust. Our portfolio covers glass jars, glass vases, glass candle holders, glass beverage dispensers, glass mason jars and glass decanters — supplying brand owners, wholesalers, hotels, restaurants, beauty brands, food & beverage companies and home decor retailers across 100+ countries. With 890+ skilled workers, a 50,000㎡ facility and 1.2 million pieces of daily output, we combine lead-free glassware production with hand-blown craftsmanship and borosilicate glass expertise.

    Standard samples delivered in 7-10 business days; custom 3D prototypes in 48 hours. Located in Zibo with 2-hour access to Qingdao Port, we ship to 100+ countries with reliable lead times.

Vertical Glass Ribbing

FAQ About Working With ZGS Glass

Five quick answers covering MOQ, sampling, customization, lead time and certifications — everything a procurement manager needs before requesting a quote.

What is your MOQ?

MOQ starts at 1000 pieces for our standard catalog designs. For fully customized shapes, capacities or colors (new mold required), MOQ is typically 10,000-20,000 pieces. Trial orders and mixed-container shipments are welcome for first-time buyers.

Do you provide free samples? How long does sampling take?

Yes. Free samples are available for standard catalog items. For custom designs, sample fees are quoted upfront and fully refundable against the bulk order. Standard samples ship within 7-10 business days; custom 3D prototypes are delivered within 48 hours for design review.

What customization options do you support?

We support five in-house decoration techniques: spray color, screen printing, laser engraving, hot stamping and decal application. Choose from 100+ in-house color cards or send a Pantone code for exact matching. All common design files are accepted (AI, PSD, PDF, CAD, STEP).

What is your production lead time and shipping window?

Sampling: 7-10 business days. Mass production: 30-60 days depending on order complexity. Located 2 hours from Qingdao Port, we ship to 100+ countries with reliable lead times per container.

Which certifications and brand audits do you hold?

BSCI, Sedex, ISO, WCA, FDA and LFGB certified. We have passed factory audits from global retail brands and ship to 100+ countries — with 22+ years of export experience.
